Hero rankings

Score is the weighted geometric mean of combat scenarios (time-to-die ÷ time-to-kill) against a self-consistent median opponent, with every hero's build solved independently at every stage. Teamfight uses only the teamfight scenarios; All-round adds picks and skirmishes. Win-rate columns are patch-current ranked match data. Click a hero for its build and purchase order.

Optimal build for every hero

Each hero's build solved independently at each stage: 9 slots of any category plus one per enemy Walker (9/9/10/11/12 by stage), at most 4 actives, ability upgrades limited to the legal points available at that stage (8/15/21/26/32).
